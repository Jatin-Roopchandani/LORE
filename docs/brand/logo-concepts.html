LORE — logo concepts

Five directions, sitting between literary and technical: a Fraunces serif wordmark, geometric graph icons. Each shows the icon on light and dark, a wordmark lockup, and favicon sizes (16, 32, 48 px). Colours are the UI's own: deep blue accent, warm amber glow.

1 · Constellation book

An open book whose story lines rise into a small star-map of linked nodes: the books become the graph. One node glows, the passage a claim traces back to.
